How to install

  1. Tap the download button above in Chrome on your phone. If Chrome says the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway.
  2. When it's done, tap Open. If you're asked, allow Chrome to Install unknown apps (Settings → turn on "Allow from this source"), then go back.
  3. Tap Install. Play Protect may warn you because the app isn't from the Play Store. Tap More details → Install anyway.
  4. Open Volume Max and allow notifications, which the boost needs.

⚠ High volume can damage your hearing and your speakers. Turn it down if the sound crackles.

Boost, Bass and Warm work on most phones, but some ignore them. If yours does, the app will tell you. To update, download again and tap Install. Your settings are kept.
